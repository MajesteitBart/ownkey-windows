# Ownkey for Windows v0.6.0

Ownkey records meetings. Right-click the tray icon, choose Meetings, and the Ownkey Meetings window opens. Microphone and call audio are recorded to your PC, with transcription enabled during recording by default. Your notes stay yours, completed passages can be corrected while the conversation continues, and the summary cites its sources. Stop finishes the remaining audio.

## Meetings

- Record the microphone, the system audio of a call, or both. Each source is
  its own track, written in five-second chunks that survive a crash. Pause
  and Stop are in the window and in the tray; Quit asks first.
- Transcription uses the same provider choices as dictation: Orukeet on this
  PC (audio never leaves the machine, word timing) or a cloud provider with
  your own key (asks before the first upload). Settings › Meetings.
- Recognition windows follow pauses and suitable speaker changes, with bounded input for long turns. Unfinished phrases from adjacent windows can stay together on screen. Disabling **Transcribe while recording** keeps the after-Stop workflow.
- Optional **live speaker changes** stream selected tracks to pyannoteAI with separate permission. Active speakers appear during recording, including overlapping voices. Pause sends generated silence to preserve identities; paused time still counts as streaming usage. Local transcription continues if the speaker connection fails.
- Stop and retry preserve completed passages and corrections. Interrupted sessions keep their saved audio and resume transcription only after an explicit retry. Paused input is discarded, including when stopping from Pause.
- My thoughts: autosaved notes with timestamps that open the transcript.
  AI never writes here, and notes stay out of AI context unless you include
  them for one question.
- Transcript: search, playback, inline corrections kept next to the
  recognition text, speaker names to confirm.
- Summary: decisions (decided, suggested or deferred), action items with
  owners left unassigned when nobody was named, open questions, all citing
  passages. Ask this meeting answers with citations and says when the record
  cannot answer. A follow-up draft is yours to copy; there is no send action.
- Speaker labels through pyannoteAI, optional: choose which tracks have
  several people (call audio, a shared microphone, or both), agree to the
  upload once, and passages come back as Speaker 1, Speaker 2, … to name.
- Export as Markdown or JSON. Delete removes everything from the library.
  Audio is kept 7 days after transcription by default.
- New meeting shows a live input meter for the selected microphone, so you
  can see the right device is picked and working before you press Start.
  The audio becomes a level and is dropped; nothing is kept.
- Meetings has its own window, hosted by the overlay process that also draws
  the pill. It is not a browser tab: no address bar, no extensions reading
  the transcript, no history entry. The default browser is only the fallback
  when that process cannot answer.

## Dictation

- While a meeting records, the dictation keys show the meeting status in the
  pill instead of recording. Meeting transcription pauses between windows
  while you dictate.
- The pill announces meeting transitions for two seconds.
- Local dictation longer than 15 seconds uses short recognition windows and returns one combined result for the existing rewrite and insertion flow.

## Settings

- Dropdowns open again. Since the redesign in 0.5.0 a click did nothing: the
  list took its font from an option written as `Segoe UI 10`, which Tk reads
  as family "Segoe" with size "UI", so building the list failed. Each
  dropdown now also shows a chevron, a hover border and a hand cursor, and
  is as tall as the text fields next to it.
- Buttons, toggles, the sidebar highlight and card corners are antialiased.
  Tk draws canvas shapes with stair-stepped edges; they are now rendered
  eight times larger and reduced.
- The page scrollbar is visible. It was one pixel wide.
- The mouse wheel scrolls the page only. It used to change whichever dropdown
  or number field was under the pointer as well, and a changed provider
  cleared the API key field.

## Known limits

- System audio capture (WASAPI loopback) was not exercised on the development
  machine. Test it before relying on call audio.
- The full private recording was processed through the incremental coordinator, and a real-time excerpt exercised Orukeet, live speaker events and Pause/Resume. Private content is excluded from the repository. A long real-time soak with two speaking hardware sources, Bluetooth routing and a human-checked accuracy reference remains outstanding; see [validation details](../MEETINGS_LIVE_PLAN.md).
- No automatic speaker labels without a pyannoteAI key.
